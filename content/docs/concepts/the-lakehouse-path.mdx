---
title: The lakehouse path
description: Bronze, Silver, Gold — how a fact earns its way into the twin, and why the layers are versioned.
---

The twin is the **Gold** layer of a lakehouse. Everything upstream of it exists to make Gold trustworthy.

## Land — Bronze

Raw source data lands **untouched** in Bronze. Nothing is interpreted at the door: the directory, the HR system, and the SQL fleet each contribute rows exactly as they describe themselves.

Bronze is deliberately dumb. Its job is to be a faithful, replayable record of what the sources said — so that a mistake downstream is a re-run, not a re-extraction.

## Conform — Silver

Records are cleaned, joined, and resolved until identities are stable. One person, one node — that is Silver.

Conformance is where the estate stops disagreeing with itself. The same human appears in the directory, in the HR system, and in an application's user table under three spellings; Silver resolves them into one identity. Snapshots are kept as SCD2 history, so "what did access look like last quarter" is a query, not an archaeology project.

Confidence scoring happens here too. A resolved identity carries the evidence it was resolved from, and a low-confidence resolution does not silently become fact.

## Publish — Gold

Conformed facts resolve into Gold: `graph_nodes` and `graph_edges`. **This graph is the twin.** Everything downstream — the traversal API, Compass, the dashboards — reads the graph, never the sources.

## Quality gates

Promotion between layers is decided by **quality gates**, not by schedule. Bad input stops at the boundary and never reaches the twin. A gate failure is visible, named, and blocks the layer advance until it is resolved.

## Versioned at every layer

The lakehouse catalog keeps history at every layer, so the twin is reproducible: last quarter's twin can be reconstructed exactly, and a finding can be re-derived against the graph as it was when the question was asked. Versioning is what turns "we think" into "we can show".

## What each layer buys you

| Layer | Contents | Question it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Raw source rows, uninterpreted | What did the sources actually say? |
| Silver | Conformed, identity-resolved, snapshotted | Who is this, really? |
| Gold | `graph_nodes` + `graph_edges` | What is connected to what? |

The mechanics of each stage — which component runs it, and the off-the-shelf product behind the codename — are in [Platform flow](/docs/architecture/platform-flow).
