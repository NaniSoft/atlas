**Forge** is the transform stage: [Spark](https://spark.apache.org/) plus [dbt](https://www.getdbt.com/), running the models that turn Bronze rows into the Gold graph.

## What Forge does

1. **Reads the cached schema.** Forge reads the current object-type schema as cached from the [DataGerry Bridge](/docs/architecture/in-house-components) sync, so it conforms the right columns — including a field like `Sensitive: bool` authored before any data flowed.
2. **Writes conformed Silver.** dbt models clean, join, and identity-resolve the raw rows, and write SCD2 snapshots so history is queryable.
3. **Scores confidence.** Identity resolution carries its evidence; a low-confidence resolution does not silently become fact.
4. **Writes Gold.** The final models publish `graph_nodes` and `graph_edges` into the lakehouse — Products, Users, and Groups as nodes; `viewed` and `memberof` as edges.

## One person, one node

Conformance is where the estate stops disagreeing with itself. The same human arrives from the directory, the HR system, and an application's user table under different spellings; Forge resolves them into a single node. That resolution is what makes a traversal answer *complete* rather than *partially complete*.

## Absence is signal

The transform stage is also where the twin becomes able to express something a report cannot: **a relationship that is missing**. A user with a `viewed` edge and no `memberof` edge is the shape of the flagship finding — and that shape only exists because conform decided what counts as a membership in the first place.
