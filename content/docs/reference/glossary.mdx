Values in **[square brackets]** are codenames; the real product is in the [integration ledger](/docs/reference/integration-ledger).

### Blast radius

A planned use case: ask what an account, a key, or a host can actually reach from where it sits, and rehearse containment against the graph you already have. Designed on the twin; needs no new connectors. **Not available today.**

### Bronze

The lakehouse layer where raw source data lands **untouched**. Nothing is interpreted at the door. See [the lakehouse path](/docs/concepts/the-lakehouse-path).

### Compass

An in-house component: the traversal UI. Renders a finding as edges, with a missing relationship drawn as a gap.

### Conform

The Silver-stage work of cleaning, joining, and resolving records until identities are stable. One person, one node.

### Edge

A relationship between two nodes in the twin: `memberof`, `viewed`, ownership, inheritance.

### Finding

An answer the twin produces that names something wrong in the estate — for example, a user who viewed a sensitive product without the membership that would back it. Readable [three ways](/docs/guides/reading-one-finding-three-ways).

### Gold

The lakehouse layer holding `graph_nodes` and `graph_edges`. **This graph is the twin.**

### IT estate

The collection of systems, people, and permissions Atlas models: directories, HR systems, databases, and applications. Atlas's first proven domain.

### Lakehouse

The versioned store — Bronze, Silver, Gold — that the twin is produced into. [Bedrock] (Nessie over Iceberg).

### Node

A thing in the estate, modelled as a vertex in the twin: a person, a group, a product, a system.

### Playbook

A scripted sequence of steps that walks a use case end to end. The playground's flagship playbook has twenty-two steps across the four phases.

### Playground

The [fully mocked, in-browser tour](https://playground.nanisoft.com) of the twin. Nothing to install, no real data. The site's single honest ask.

### Quality gate

The check that decides whether a layer advance happens. Bad input stops at the boundary and never reaches the twin.

### SCD2

Slowly-changing dimension, type 2: keeping history as rows with validity ranges, so "what did access look like last quarter" is a query.

### Scout

An in-house component: connectors for internal source systems no off-the-shelf catalog covers.

### Sensitive product

A product node carrying an authored `Sensitive: bool` field — the definitional hinge of the flagship audit, authored before any data flows.

### Silver

The lakehouse layer of conformed, identity-resolved tables with SCD2 snapshots.

### Stale and unused access

A planned use case: find memberships nobody remembers granting and privileges nobody has exercised, and feed clean-up work with evidence instead of anecdotes. **Not available today.**

### Traversal

Answering a question by walking edges from a starting node, rather than by joining exports. The twin's only way of answering.

### Twin

The living graph that models the estate — produced, versioned, and queryable. See [the twin](/docs/concepts/the-twin).

### Use case

A question the twin can answer as a product surface. One is available today ([access traversal](/docs/guides/access-traversal)); two are planned. Statuses are always stated, never implied.
