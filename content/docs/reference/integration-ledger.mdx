Every off-the-shelf product below runs **unmodified** — integrated through its APIs, configured, never forked. Codenamed entries are the real product shown beneath the codename, not a reimplementation of it.

The first eight run under a codename; the second eight run under their own name. Where the two columns are identical there was never a seam to draw.

Where each one sits in the pipeline is in [Platform flow](/docs/architecture/platform-flow); what each stage does is in its own [architecture page](/docs/architecture/orchestration).

## Built in-house — four components

| Component | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Atlas** | The core engine: traversal API, policy enforcement, audit log. |
| **Compass** | The traversal UI: explore the twin as a graph. |
| **DataGerry Bridge** | Glue that syncs authored schema into the lakehouse and the engine. |
| **Scout** | Connectors for internal systems no catalog covers. |

## Reading the ledger

- **The codename is a seam, not a disguise.** It is why the platform's contracts can survive a product being swapped underneath, and why every codenamed entry publishes the real product rather than hiding it.
- **"Custom (4)" is a schema surface, not a storage count.** DataGerry is off-the-shelf; the in-house Bridge is what carries its authored schema into the lakehouse and the engine.
- **Dotted edges are not data flow.** Provisioning, deploy, secrets, and observation are platform concerns, not facts about the estate.
